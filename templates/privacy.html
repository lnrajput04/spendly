Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic information such as your name and email address. As you use the Service, we also collect the expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to operate and improve the Service: authenticating your account, displaying your expenses, and generating summaries and category breakdowns. We do not use your financial data for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. We take reasonable measures to protect your data from unauthorized access. You may request deletion of your data at any time by removing your account or contacting us.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell your personal or financial data to third parties. We may rely on third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting services) to operate the Service, and those providers only process data as needed to keep the Service running.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website.
